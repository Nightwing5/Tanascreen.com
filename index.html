ACCESSIBILITY APPROACH

Clear. Flexible. Player-first.

Accessibility is an ongoing design practice, not a one-size-fits-all setting.

01

Show what to do

Use demonstrations, icons, examples, and clear visual cues alongside text.

02

Break it into steps

Introduce mechanics gradually and allow practice without unnecessary pressure.

03

Offer choices

Where practical, include adjustable difficulty, readable interfaces, captions, and sensory settings.

04

Listen and improve

Seek feedback from neurodivergent players, educators, and families.

We aim to design more accessible experiences; we do not claim every game suits every person or replaces educational or support services.
